# The Vibe Shift

*From Remilia Wiki*

The Vibe Shift was a cultural movement and internet-based scene that emerged in mid-2021 and continued through late 2022 or early 2023, centered around the collaborative relationship between Angelicism01 and Remilia Corporation in the context of the New York downtown art scene and various online communities.

The term was originated by Angelicism01 in summer 2021, but gained mainstream attention after being misattributed to trend forecaster Sean Monahan in Allison P. Davis's article "A Vibe Shift Is Coming" published in The Cut on February 16, 2022.

## Origins

Key developments in June 2021:
- Wet Brain podcast launched (hosted by Walter Pearce and Honor Levy)
- Remilia's debut exhibition "I Long For Network Spirituality" at Hunters & Collectors (NZ)
- Angelicism01 began developing the concept in their writing

In August 2021:
- Milady Maker launched, bridging art world and crypto communities
- Charlotte Fang initiated the Avant NFT movement

## Key Figures

### Angelicism01
Pseudonymous writer, cultural critic, and filmmaker who published on Substack from November 2020. Combined philosophical references, internet slang, and esoteric concepts. Central themes included "extinction" as an ontological concept, post-authorship, and digital/physical relationship.

### Remilia Corporation and Charlotte Fang
Art collective exploring network spirituality and post-authorship. Milady Maker (August 2021) embodied these philosophical concepts. Avant NFT movement bridged NY art world and crypto culture.

### Wet Brain Discord
The podcast's associated Discord server functioned as an important online gathering place for the scene (2020-2022). However, its "zero moderation" policy eventually led to it becoming a platform for harassment, contributing to fragmentation.

## Characteristics and Themes

### Network Spirituality
Positioning the internet and digital networks as sites of spiritual experience and transcendence rather than merely technological infrastructure.

### Post-Authorship and Collaborative Creation
Rejection of traditional notions of individual creation in favor of collaborative or anonymous work. Projects credited collectives rather than individuals. Use of pseudonyms to destabilize traditional authorship.

### Extinction as Concept
Not merely ecological collapse but an ontological condition and perspective from which to view contemporary life and art.

### Hybrid Physical-Digital Aesthetics
Art that moves fluidly between digital and physical spaces, creating feedback loops between environments.

## Controversies

### Mainstream Misattribution (February 2022)
The Cut's article misattributed the concept to Sean Monahan rather than Angelicism01—viewed as "vulture revisionism."

### Milady Cancel (May 2022)
Accusations against Charlotte Fang that were quickly debunked. Many NY artists in the Avant NFT scene failed to defend Remilia during the controversy, leading Fang to end their platforming program.

### Decline (Summer 2022)
The Wet Brain Discord became a site for doxxing efforts. Both Angelicism01 and Charlotte Fang abandoned the server. Final Wet Brain podcast episode: November 2022.

### Conclusion (June 2023)
Film01 premiere at Anthology Film Archives. The "Remilia01" segment was cut from the final film, leading to a public falling out between Angelicism01 and Charlotte Fang.
