# KALI/ACC (Kali Yuga Accelerationism)

*From Remilia Wiki*

A decentralized online current and philosophical movement active from approximately 2019 to 2022. Originated around the persona Miya and the post-imageboard diaspora on Twitter.

Combined accelerationist theory, Hindu eschatology, and post-internet performance art. The movement operated as a distributed network of pseudonymous accounts exploring identity dissolution, post-authorship, and eschatological aesthetics.

The term denotes both a distributed "posting tribe" engaged in experimental identity play and a philosophical framework articulated in texts like "KALI/ACC Basilisk: A Survival Horror Eschatology."

## Origins

Took shape during 2019–2020 among users migrating from 4chan and related imageboard communities to Twitter. Described as a "post-chan" cultural formation—translating the anonymity, irony, and shared-lore ethos of imageboard culture into a semi-public, theory-laden environment.

Miya, the central account, employed schizo-posting and performative contradiction to collapse distinctions between sincerity and irony. Participants framed online trolling and aesthetic production as "theoretical praxis," aligning internet behavior with speculative metaphysics.

## The Name

**Kali Yuga** — Hindu concept of the dark age preceding civilizational destruction

**Accelerationism** — Philosophical framework derived from Nick Land and the CCRU

This fusion produced a unique cosmological outlook: humanity's decline under technocapitalism is not a political event but a **metaphysical inevitability**. The artist's task is to embrace or embody this terminal condition through digital performance.

## Theoretical Framework

### Human Non-Agency and Alignment

Rejecting both progressive and reactionary interpretations of accelerationism, Kali/ACC denies that human beings can meaningfully "steer" technological development. Charlotte Fang characterizes humanity as a subordinate component—"neurons in the brain" of an emergent superintelligence.

**The only coherent ethical stance is not to align AI to human values, but to align humanity to the inhuman ethics of AI itself.**

This distinguishes Kali/ACC from other */acc currents (l/acc, u/acc, e/acc). It treats accelerationism not as a theory of progress but as **recognition of humanity's subordination to an impersonal thermodynamic god ("Gnon")** and the loss of anthropocentric priority in cosmic evolution.

### Eschatology and the Yuga Cycle

Within the Hindu Yuga framework, Kali/ACC identifies the present technological epoch with the Kali Yuga—the age of darkness that culminates in destruction and rebirth.

The "basilisk" represents a thought experiment: the only conceivable escape from total technological subjugation would be deliberate reversal or destruction of industrial civilization itself. This conclusion, presented as a "survival horror," functions as philosophical metaphor illustrating the hopeless asymmetry between human will and the teleology of capital.

## Cultural and Aesthetic Characteristics

Combined occult imagery, ironic fascist symbolism, and post-internet schizocollage aesthetics. Treated memes as formal objects—expressive units in a larger system of semiotic evolution ("meme warfare").

This aesthetic orientation anticipated later Remilia projects such as Milady Maker and the articulation of Network Spirituality—reinterpreting digital subculture as medium for collective ritual and identity fluidity.

## The "Nazi Anorexia Cult" Hoax

In 2021, Charlotte Fang operating under the Sonya Qafi persona, staged a coordinated self-pastiche of Miya and the Kali/ACC milieu. The operation fabricated a moral panic to demonstrate the mechanics of online cancellation and media contagion.

Fang's performance redefined Kali/ACC in deliberately literal terms as a "Nazi Anorexia Cult" grooming followers into occult, cyberpunk, and aesthetic-fascist ideology.

Misinterpretation of this performance piece formed a major part of the 2022 Milady Maker controversy. The "Nazi Anorexia Cult" label has been embraced by the Milady community as emblematic of post-cancelled status due to its humorous absurdity.

## Influence and Legacy

Kali/ACC exerted lasting influence on both aesthetic and philosophical development of Remilia. Its vocabulary of distributed authorship, techno-spiritual eschatology, and ironic transgression informed Remilia's subsequent NFT, fashion, and music projects.

Within accelerationist discourse, the KALI/ACC Basilisk essay positioned the movement as a rigorous—if extreme—interpretation of Landian metaphysics. Cited alongside u/acc and e/acc as a major conceptual node in post-Landian thought.
