# The Cathedral

*From Remilia Wiki*

Political concept originally developed by Curtis Yarvin (writing as Mencius Moldbug). Describes a decentralized ideological apparatus consisting of academia, media, and bureaucracy that functions as a secular theocracy to propagate progressive orthodoxy.

Within Remilia's theoretical system, The Cathedral concept has been integrated with Fang's analyses of State Propaganda Complex and Democracy Breeds for Control to examine how cultural production shapes political consciousness.

## Origins

Originated in the writings of Curtis Yarvin in the late 2000s:

> "[The Cathedral is] the self-organizing consensus of universities and the press" which functions as "a secular theocracy."

The concept became influential in neoreactionary circles. Within Remilia, Fang incorporated aspects of The Cathedral analysis while developing their own concepts, creating a synthesis that emphasizes aesthetic and spiritual dimensions alongside political analysis.

## Core Mechanisms

### Consensus Manufacturing
Shapes perception by determining which ideas are considered reasonable, moderate, or extreme. Occurs not through explicit censorship but through establishment of intellectual prestige hierarchies and subtle marginalization of non-conforming perspectives.

### Status Competition
Career advancement, social recognition, and cultural capital allocated to those who effectively internalize and propagate Cathedral values. Self-reinforcing dynamics where ambitious individuals compete to demonstrate alignment with approved positions.

### Linguistic Control
Management of language—establishing which terms are acceptable, which concepts deserve names, and which distinctions may be articulated. By controlling vocabulary, The Cathedral shapes the boundaries of conceivable thought.

### Aesthetic Enforcement
Maintains power partly through aesthetic judgments that align beauty with approved politics. Art that challenges Cathedral values is categorized as ugly, regressive, or dangerous, while art that reinforces those values receives institutional support and critical acclaim.

## Relationship to Other Remilia Concepts

**State Propaganda Complex:** Examines the mechanisms through which ideological production shapes population behavior. The Cathedral identifies the producers of consensus reality; the propaganda complex describes how this reality is disseminated and internalized.

**Democracy Breeds for Control:** Addresses biological and demographic dimensions of social control, how democratic systems select for traits that make populations more governable. The Cathedral explains how cultural institutions shape the ideological environment for this selection.

**Transcendental Turn:** Represents a cultural response to The Cathedral's materialist framework. Renewed interest in faith, beauty, and transcendence as alternatives to Cathedral-approved forms of meaning. Signifies both diagnosis of The Cathedral's spiritual exhaustion and prescription for cultural renewal.
