# Redacted Remilio Babies

*From Remilia Wiki*

Redacted Remilio Babies (commonly known as Remilio) is a collection of 10,000 generative profile picture NFTs created by Remilia Corporation and launched on the Ethereum blockchain in August 2022.

The collection extends the neochibi aesthetic established by Milady Maker, repositioning it with what Remilia termed "youthful J.I.T. energy, schizophrenic reactionary aesthetics, and digital sales terrorism." Described as the canonical "little brother" to Milady.

## Design Philosophy

Where Milady explores Japanese street fashion subcultures through a feminine aesthetic of spiritual purity, Remilio adopts a masculine counterpart aesthetic characterized by:
- Aspirational finance culture
- Internet reactionary humor  
- Adolescent irreverence

The project emerged just three months after the May 2022 Milady cancellation controversy, serving as Remilia's first major release in the post-cancelled era.

## Key Features

### Friends System
Over 100 small mascot characters placed in corners, hidden beyond what displays in a circular profile picture format. Provides easter eggs and humorous citations.

### Overlays
A distinctive feature including the infamous "RACISM" overlay, which Remilia described as preemptively lampooning an inevitable cancellation attempt. Self-aware embrace of controversy as aesthetic material.

### Swag Score
Similar to Milady's Drip Score system, each Remilio receives a "Swag Score" evaluating aesthetic coherence and trait combinations.

## Cultural Positioning

Remilio's conceptual framework deliberately contrasts with Milady's aesthetic of feminine spiritual purity:
- Milady: secluded, innocent, inward-focused
- Remilio: outward-directed, aggressive humor, boyish edginess

It acts as a receptacle for the boyish crudeness that exists within Remilia's community but sits uncomfortably alongside Milady's pursuit of aesthetic purity.

The collection operates in what Remilia termed "lucid metairony."
