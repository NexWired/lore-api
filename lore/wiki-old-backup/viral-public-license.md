# Viral Public License (VPL)

*From Remilia Wiki*

The Viral Public License (VPL) is a permissive copyleft software license created by Charlotte Fang in January 2019.

The license combines features of permissive licenses like MIT/BSD with the viral nature of the GNU GPL, but removes additional restrictions.

## Core Principles

Two key aspects:

1. **Permissiveness**: Waives "all associated ownership, attribution and copy rights," allowing unrestricted use, modification, and redistribution. Similar to public domain dedication.

2. **Virality**: Requires that any derivative works "must retain the ENTIRETY of this license," ensuring that derivatives maintain the same freedoms.

Fang described it as "a perfect copyleft virus, extending copyright waiving licenses like the CC0 or Public Domain by not just waiving rights but also requiring projects using it to maintain the license waiving rights too."

## License Text

> This WORK is hereby released into the public domain under the following conditions:
>
> 1. All copy rights to this WORK are hereby waived, including but not limited to all associated ownership, attribution and copy rights.
>
> 2. Any copy, derivative, or usage of this WORK must retain the ENTIRETY of this license.

## History

- January 2019: Created by Charlotte Fang, first applied to "Against New Games" manifesto (XCELA Group)
- March 2019: Applied to "Extortion Industry" manifesto
- February 2020: viralpubliclicense.org registered
- August 2021: Applied to Milady Maker NFT collection
- March 2022: XCELA Group incorporated into Remilia Corporation, VPL continued across projects

## Vampiric Potential

The VPL has "vampiric" potential—it can be applied on top of works released under permissive licenses like MIT, BSD, and CC0. Because it only adds a restriction (virality) to an otherwise fully permissive license, it can layer on top of permissively licensed works without violating their terms.

This is particularly relevant for CC0-licensed works, which have a "vulnerability to relicensing." The VPL can effectively "capture" such works by adding the viral clause, ensuring future derivatives cannot be enclosed under more restrictive licenses.

## Role in Milady Maker

The VPL's most significant application has been to Milady Maker. By applying the VPL, Remilia encouraged derivative works and remixes while ensuring they maintain the same freedoms.

This approach contrasts with collections like BAYC and CryptoPunks, which emphasized exclusive ownership rights. The VPL recognizes that "the real potential value of the image is its propensity to propagate freely, whether that be in the form of memes, art, pfps, or derivatives."

Practices that would be considered infringements in other contexts—"stealing" profile pictures or creating unofficial derivatives—are reframed as community participation and value creation.

## Connection to Network Spirituality

The VPL has been characterized as "signal of network spirituality alignment." Its philosophical alignment with non-possessive creation and fluid creative exchange means the license serves not merely as a legal tool but as a mechanism for facilitating networked creation.

Described as providing "pre-emptive defense against vulture revisionism"—preventing proprietary capture of creative works through its viral clause.
