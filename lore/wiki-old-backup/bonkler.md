# Bonkler

*From Remilia Wiki*

Bonkler was an experimental on-chain generative art and auction project by Remilia Corporation, launched in 2023 on Ethereum. Designed as a "finance art" counterpart to Remilia's avatar projects.

Each day a new artwork was minted, combining ritualized scarcity with experimental game design before being halted by Bonkler 9/11 in September 2023.

## Concept

Conceived by Charlotte Fang in August 2022 as a response to Nouns DAO's structural flaws:

> "Nouns is an experiment in punishing its earliest investors."
> — Charlotte Fang, A Critical Review of Nouns DAO, 2022

Fang framed Bonkler as a conceptual fork that would resolve these flaws while preserving the ritual of daily generativity—expanded into an experiment in trust, faith, and myth-building.

The project was described as "a World Fair for the internet" and "a year-long prank on price discovery."

## Mechanics

- Sequential English auctions, 23 hours each
- 15-minute extension if bid in final 15 minutes
- Reserve-backed "shares" proportional to final sale price
- Holders can burn NFT to redeem ETH from Treasury
- 30% fee to Remilia, rest to Treasury

## Artistic Style

Described as "citational collage-pastiche on a Bionicle base" with:
- Schizomorphic aesthetic lineage
- Hypercitation and toy-shelf artifact sensibilities
- Algorithmic forms balancing structural repetition with surreal variation

## Bonkler 9/11

On September 11, 2023, mints were halted when a Remilia developer diverted approximately $1 million in fees through an unauthorized contract. Community dubbed the event "Bonkler 9/11."

The crisis consolidated Fang's leadership and transformed Bonklers into cult artifacts.

## Legacy

- Response to Nouns DAO's deflationary flaws
- Serialized generative minting as cultural ritual
- Reserve-backed NFT mechanics
- Symbol of vulnerability and resilience after Bonkler 9/11
