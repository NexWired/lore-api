# Effective Accelerationism (e/acc)

*From Remilia Wiki*

A 21st-century ideological movement advocating for unrestricted technological progress, particularly in AI development. Emerged in 2022, gained significant attention in 2023 when high-profile Silicon Valley figures endorsed it.

Related concepts: Accelerationist Realism, KALI/ACC, Nick Land

## Origins

Emerged in May 2022 through a newsletter by four pseudonymous authors (@BasedBeffJezos, @bayeslord, @zestular, @creatine_cycle). The name is a portmanteau of "effective altruism" and "accelerationism."

In December 2023, Forbes revealed @BasedBeffJezos was Guillaume Verdon, a former Google quantum computing engineer.

## Core Principles

**Technological maximalism:** Unrestricted technological development, particularly AGI. Rejects attempts to slow or regulate AI progress.

**Kardashev scale ascension:** Aim for civilization to "climb the Kardashev gradient" by maximizing energy usage.

**Thermodynamic foundations:** Grounded in Jeremy England's theories on entropy. The universe aims to increase entropy; life achieves this. Spreading life and increasing energy fulfills cosmological purpose.

**Market fundamentalism:** AGI will be most "aligned" if multiple AGIs compete in free markets rather than under centralized control.

## Critique from Remilia / Accelerationist Realism

Charlotte Fang and Chloe21e8 (Remilia ecosystem) have directly challenged e/acc as a "sanitized" version of accelerationism that fails to embrace its radical implications.

### The "Defanged Kali/ACC" Critique

> "e/acc will never succeed milady bc it's just presenting a defanged kali/acc for LinkedIn."
> — Charlotte Fang

Fang describes e/acc as a "diluted, downstream co-option" lacking "serious discourse or convictions."

### Human Non-Agency Critique

The core philosophical disagreement centers on human agency.

**Accelerationist Realism** (from KALI/ACC Basilisk essay): Capitalism is "the thermodynamic ruling entity of all material dynamics" and "nature's god." Human agency is fundamentally subordinate to technological and capitalist forces.

**E/acc** reportedly stated: "HUMANS HAVE AGENCY RIGHT NOW. WE CAN AFFECT THE ADVENT OF THE INFLECTION IN THIS PROCESS."

Accelerationist Realism counters: "Any attributions of human agency are delusions at worst and hubris at best when our every scale action is being routed by a self-assembling thermodynamic god. Human non-agency under non-negotiable capitalism is the blackpill of accelerationism."

Fang claims that when challenged about thermodynamic principles in Twitter spaces, e/acc leaders "walked it back almost immediately, the moment the first normie pointed out the abyss sounds scary."

### Qualified Support

Despite criticisms, Fang has acknowledged qualified support:

> "In terms of political factions, I do support e/acc as the Baycel reaction against Alignment, which very clearly is designed to seize freedoms. e/acc could certainly be more radical, more honest and higher conviction, but then they would be milady."

## Relation to Milady

When OpenAI's Roon claimed e/acc is the "spiritual successor of milady," Fang rejected this:

> "e/acc will never succeed milady bc it's just presenting a defanged kali/acc for linkedin... Milady is the radical annihilistic rhizomatic network naturally self-organized through a true understanding of landian acceleration."

### The Milady Approach: Radical Love

From the Milady community, an alternative framing:

> "E/acc, the incentive to 'push' acceleration, is abjectly laughable. Ride a dragon, assuming to direct it requires dissonance... progression through aspiration is hardwired, you are a tool of nature against your own interest."

> "Enter Milady, a simple attempt to flag a group awareness of the contradictory nature of this quagmire ensnaring the possible end of history. Holding hands as the comet fills sky, laughing, making fun. Facing infinity together."

Milady's approach, known as Radical Love, accepts the abyss rather than trying to control it—embracing community and humor in the face of technological inevitability rather than claiming agency over it.
