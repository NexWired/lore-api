# Charlotte Fang

*From Remilia Wiki*

Conceptual artist, writer, and theorist. Founder and creative director of Remilia Corporation. Known for Milady Maker, Network Spirituality, and post-authorship philosophy.

**Website:** goldenlight.mirror.xyz

## Background and Emergence

Charlotte Fang first appeared in the digital art scene in late 2020, formally announcing Remilia Corporation in January 2021. Initially presented as a Chinese fashion student at Central Saint Martins.

Fang's artistic lineage connects to earlier online personas including Miya Black Hearted Cyber Angel Baby (2016–2020), which developed experimental theory-fiction exploring accelerationism and posthuman identity. The connection remained deliberately ambiguous, consistent with Remilia's post-authorship philosophy that treats digital identities as fluid constructs.

In May 2022, amid controversy, Fang published "Admin Reveal: I said I'm just a vessel bro," acknowledging that Krishna Pandit Okhandiar was the legal identity behind the projects. Fang has continued to function as a distinct artistic identity and public face of Remilia Corporation.

## Artistic Approach

Fang investigates how digital networks facilitate cultural transformation and spiritual exchange, treating technology not merely as a distribution tool but as a medium for collective consciousness.

Influences:
- Accelerationist philosophy
- Cyberpunk tradition
- CCRU (Cybernetic Culture Research Unit)
- Andy Warhol's Factory collaborative model

### Key Philosophical Frameworks

**Network Spirituality:** Lucid participation in digital networks enables users to tap into collective consciousness from which greater truths can be derived.

> "Transcendental posting, performative identity & post-authorship are a distinct archetype of engagement you'll find power users independently arrive on in any of the deep wells of internet culture."

**Post-Authorship:** Dissolution of individual authorship in favor of collective creative consciousness. Remixing as the natural mode of art-making online.

> "Accepting the death of authorship recognizes and rejects the social mores that hamper an ability to freely use, modify and propagate artist's work."

**Transcendental Turn:** Rejects nihilism and atheistic materialism in favor of seeking eternal truth and beauty through lucid artmaking, with the artist functioning as conduit for forces beyond individual consciousness.

**Neo-Orientalism:** Ongoing cultural dialogue between Eastern and Western underground scenes where subcultural aesthetics cross-pollinate.

**Left-Hand Style:** Deliberately non-technically competent visual approach that challenges technical virtuosity as prerequisite for artistic validity.

**Pseudophilosophy:** Methodical structuring of hypothetical philosophical paradigms using fringe ideas as creative resources rather than truth claims.

## Remilia Corporation

Founded in 2021, named after Remilia Scarlet from Touhou Project. Operates primarily within remote group chats with pseudonymous members.

> "A fellowship of pseudonymous net art extremists."

Remilia has maintained independence from venture capital investment, which Fang articulates as essential for preserving creative autonomy.

## Major Projects

### Milady Maker (2021)
10,000 generative PFP NFTs in "neochibi" style influenced by Tokyo street fashion and Y2K aesthetics. Synthesizes Fang's core principles:
- Neo-Orientalism through Japanese-inspired aesthetic
- Left-Hand Style in deliberately childlike drawing
- Hypercitational Practice with dense cultural references
- Post-Authorship in collaborative creation

Adopted by prominent figures including Vitalik Buterin. As of 2025, one of the most traded NFT collections globally.

### Other Projects
- Redacted Remilio Babies (2022) — provocative companion collection
- Bonkler (2023) — experimental finance art with unique auction mechanics
- Milady Rave series (2022-ongoing) — underground events with hyperpop, breakcore, fashion pop-ups
- CHEESEWORLD (2024-ongoing) — collaborative AI art using custom DALL-E 3 model

## Controversy (May 2022)

Twitter user 0xngmi published a thread demonstrating connections between Fang and Miya persona. In response, Fang published "Admin Reveal: I said I'm just a vessel bro":

> "Truthfully, I saw Miya and Sonya more as preliminary studies, not something I ever expected to see a serious audience beyond the participating subjects."

> "What makes this controversy particularly fascinating is how it exemplifies the very mechanisms the project had been examining: the weaponization of identity, the collapse of nuance in digital discourse, and the impossibility of maintaining consistent selfhood across network contexts."

Milady Maker recovered its market value. The anniversary is commemorated as the "cancelversary."
