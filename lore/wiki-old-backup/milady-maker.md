# Milady Maker

*From Remilia Wiki*

**Parent group:** Remilia Corporation  
**Type:** Generative PFP (10,000 supply)  
**Blockchain:** Ethereum (ERC-721)  
**Mint date:** August 25, 2021 (sold out April 19, 2022)  
**Mint price:** 0.06–0.08 ETH

Milady Maker is a collection of 10,000 generative profile picture NFTs featuring anime-style avatars in a neochibi aesthetic inspired by Tokyo street fashion subcultures. As Remilia's flagship project, it became one of the culturally significant PFP collections from the 2021 NFT cycle.

## Origins

Developed by Remilia Corporation, led by Charlotte Fang. Emerged during summer 2021 as Remilia became integrated with the Vibe Shift scene. Drew from Fang's earlier work with Miya and the Kali/ACC milieu.

Positioned as an experiment in decentralized branding and network-native identity—the collective's first significant crossover between post-internet art and cryptocurrency communities.

## Design Philosophy

Five primary Tokyo fashion subcultures:
- Lolita
- Harajuku  
- Gyaru
- Hypebeast
- Prep

**Neochibi style:** Contemporary reinterpretation of traditional chibi aesthetics maintaining simplified proportions while incorporating greater visual complexity. Inspired by Takashi Murakami's Chibi Squishy and FRUITS magazine. Described as "affective flatness"—deliberate emotional ambiguity allowing each Milady to function as blank canvas for projected identity.

**Chain roll function:** Randomized cosmetics organized into style themes with chance of consecutive generation sticking to same theme, producing organic aesthetic coherency.

### Drip Score System

Each Milady receives a score (12-67) and letter grade (C to SS) based on aesthetic coherence and trait harmony rather than simple rarity. Rewards aesthetic judgment and cultural literacy over statistical scarcity.

## Theoretical Framework

**Post-Authorship:** Creation attributed to Remilia Corporation as entity, not individual artists. Adopting a Milady as PFP = participation in collective identity and cultural movement.

**Network Spirituality:** Milady holders developed practices of collective identity formation, shared aesthetic sensibility, and communal meaning-making transcending simple financial speculation. NFTs as vehicles for spiritual community.

**Viral Public License:** All branding, assets, and NFTs are copylefted under VPL, enabling open remix culture.

## The Milady Cancel (May 2022)

Anonymous developer 0xngmi published viral Twitter thread with extreme allegations against Charlotte Fang. The controversy centered on a 2021 performance piece where Fang, operating under the Sonya Qafi persona, had staged a coordinated self-pastiche redefining Kali/ACC as a "Nazi Anorexia Cult"—an operation intended to demonstrate the mechanics of online cancellation.

Fang published "Cancel Miya to Me or I'll Fucking Kill You" defending the artistic integrity while stepping back from active project leadership.

Within weeks, claims were extensively debunked. The cancellation paradoxically strengthened Remilia's position—market value recovered and surpassed pre-controversy levels. The collective emerged with a "post-cancelled" identity central to its mythology.

Anniversary commemorated as the "cancelversary." The Nazi Anorexia Cult label embraced as emblematic of post-cancelled status due to its humorous absurdity.

## Later Recognition

**May 2023:** Elon Musk tweeted Milady image with "There is no meme. I love you." Floor price jumped from 3.75 ETH to over 7 ETH within hours.

**2023:** NFT Project of the Year at inaugural Binance Awards.

**January 2025:** Vitalik Buterin purchased Milady #9286 for 5.82 ETH and used it as Twitter profile picture during what observers characterized as "wartime mode" for Ethereum leadership.

## Legacy

Demonstrated that NFT projects could function as vehicles for serious artistic and philosophical investigation while simultaneously operating as viral meme culture.

Established parameters for "Avant NFT"—blockchain-based art prioritizing conceptual depth and cultural meaning over purely technical or financial considerations.

As of 2025, maintains market capitalization in the hundreds of millions and continues functioning as central node in crypto culture discourse.
