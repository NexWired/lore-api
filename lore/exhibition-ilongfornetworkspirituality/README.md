# I Long For Network Spirituality - Exhibition Archive

Source: https://ilongfornetworkspirituality.net

An archive of Remilia Collective's debut show "I Long For Network Spirituality" featuring artwork by:
- Mara Barl
- Ilyena Nienel
- Atrpntime
- Milady Sonora
- Fodkorp
- Chensi Fang

Simultaneously presented at Hunters & Collectors, New Zealand and online.

The opening party featured an on-site installation of the early alpha of Remilia Chat on a Thinkpad X11 displaying its real time anonymous chatroom with a simultaneous livestream of the gallery.
