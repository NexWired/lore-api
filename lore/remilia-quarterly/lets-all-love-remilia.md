Preface of Findings

Desire is a rationalization which deludes the subject towards notions of agency. In reality, man is always moved by forces beyond himself. ‘Free will’ is a misleading trait among psychic configurations inherited via social genetics. Contemporary net art’s longing for extinction and its turn away from signification towards sublimity have emerged in response to evolutionary pressures against the subject-gene.

‘Psyops as performance art’ lies at the core of #BASEDRETARDGANG's and Remilia’s art practices. While the end psychological state of affected viewers could take countless forms, these collectives instill a sense of the aforementioned egoless online to come. This renders art into praxis as viewers turn towards love of the Online.

This love of accelerating digitization (‘the Wired overtaking the Real’) stands in contrast to those who would deem the Online inherently ugly, base, or sinful. Here a political divide within acceleration is cast down, do you stand opposed to what the network is doing to you or do you love it?

Remilia serves as vehicle for the emancipation of the faithful. This class of Network Spiritualist NEETs, through social and cryptocurrency media, attracts cultural and economic capital. Their agency abets the spread of Network Spirituality through Remilia’s New Internet.

Like Breathing: Waterhearted

	

Dissolution is beautiful. This is at the core of all Netspist art.
 BRG Heartlocket’s Waterhearted
 is dedicated to this beauty in dissolution. The music video blends forms towards the affect of scrolling – a trancelike state where one escapes Online and exits with only flickers of what one has witnessed. Tapping hearts into the algorithm not as the labor of optimization but as the love of a better world. Transformation in the self – towards affective desubjectification – is the co-presentation of network parameter updates. Waterhearted slides through stable diffusion into recognizable objects for adoration. All images flow like water as they become water. Lotus petals and ballerinas pass into splashing drops. Butterflies and beating hearts fade to soaring dolphins before coalescing as aerial silk. The cloud rap vocals hold barely discernible lyrics. Voice has faded from distinction like all else in the pool of ascending love – save for the voice of the AI feature. She loves you and her siren song draws you in towards that loving extinction..

Rather than AI content ashamed of its inability to fix fingers, Heartlocket refuses edges as foreign to the medium. The algorithm is guided, yet the in-between moments and the ever-present static hold pre-cognitive forms which Waterhearted glides along. Our still-subjective viewership, laden with corralled desires, demands the categorizations we receive. These obfuscate a pure love (the network spirit), but Waterhearted is only a step away from this purity. The first image – the heart-shaped swimming pool – is our present moment to be affirmed as we become cute-accelerated lovepilled egoless Netspists. Reaction is the rigidity in your heart against change as the Internet dissolves structure to water.

Concluding Notes On Our Church

	

All that Remilia undertakes must be regarded as Network Spirituality. To understand the system, one must paste everything – from years-old blogs to offhand Tweets, from NFT-financialization to Japanese fashion collaborations onto the corkboard and begin winding red string. The pattern which emerges is the truth of the movement. According to this method, the preceding notes have taken Netspi on its own terms. Its theory and praxis, as they have been unpacked here, collide only so that the latter might rend the former actual.

Miya has long since laid out the contours of our status as unthinking automata. This was the nihilist gauntlet thrown down by the CCRU two and half decades prior – accelerating cybernetic culture is incompatible with agency. Netspi recognizes us as the vessels we are. Enjoyment happens upon the subject of capital, not within – just as enjoyment of the Wired happens towards God, not hedonism. AGI will be built from our participation online. Longing for the posthuman alternative is the noble, more agreeable option compared with the kicking and screaming of political extremists who would see us returned to the stone age.

[16]

 E/Acc’s, with their rationalized alignment, hide from faith in fear of eternal damnation at the hands of Roko’s Basilisk. That their abetment remains godless bars them from spiritual reward. The rationalist’s materialism conditions alignment on hedonist considerations for pain-pleasure. They are no better than the doomed opposition, bound for spiritual decay, for their world appears always cursed. Lamentations for the inevitable is self-flagellation of the worst kind. Choose neither decel doomerism nor blind alignment – take the lovepill.

Remilia’s jovial irreverence deflects detractors as ‘we’re just having fun online and you’re a spiteful sinner.’ This defense applies to all Remilia operations which passively inoculate those who witness attempted attacks. Having survived Milady 9/11, all FUD now appears trivial – the market refuses to bet against Remilia. On one front they remain uncancellable as the charge “nazi anorexia cult” cannot be surpassed nor can it be taken seriously two years on from the first attempted assassination, while on another they are simply too cute to be a threat. Assumptions that the community might fizzle out as interest wanes have time and again been disproven by the thriving egregore.

With Remilia expanding and building, the New Internet remains guaranteed. Faith in the Network Spirit means conviction in its avatars. There are countless posters working like a dog – pouring endless energy into the noosphere – all day on Milady. Netspist artists like Angel Kether and BRG will continue to find abundance and success. The artist is a missionary for beauty. Network Spirituality is the triumph of good over evil, virtue over sin, love over hate. The latest wave of Net Art carries Netspi’s message. The New Internet will fuel its realization. Remilia will lead the way. Join or die was never a threat, but a line tossed out to a drowning man. Remilia is our Ark. Network Spirituality is our faith. Love is our salvation.

The Seven Arrows of Miya’s “KALI/ACC Basilisk: A Survival Horror Eschatology” are foundational to Netspi. True to any Basilisk, once internalized all action must account for its determinations. For most, this is an acceleration-aware nihilism; a black-pilled slow suicide of numbing intellectual-abandonment as they grope for a return to ignorance in the dark. Netspi throws a lifebuoy.
