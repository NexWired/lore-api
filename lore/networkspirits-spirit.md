# Network Spirituality - Definition & Philosophy
Source: networkspirits.ltd/spirit
Scraped: 2026-02-06

## Origin
Term coined by FODCOM (Remilia artist), introduced via "I Long for Network Spirituality" show.

## Core Concepts

### The Wired (Serial Experiments Lain, 1998)
Virtual realm containing sum of human communication & networks. Series explores consciousness, perception, nature of reality. Features concept of unconscious communication between people & machines without physical interface - a spiritual network.

### Noosphere (Pierre Teilhard de Chardin)
The "thinking layer" - highest stage of biospheric development. Emerges through interaction of human minds. As mankind organizes in more complex social networks, noosphere grows in awareness.

### Key Synthesis
"When we become collectively conscious that our internal & external existence is spiritually unified with the network, that our mind & body are egoless, & the content we create on the internet is with Truth & Beauty, we become Network Spiritual."

## Practices
- Altering 'dynamical state of mind' with specific contexts & experiences
- Psychotherapy, Mindfulness, Dreaming, Hypnogogic States, Active Imagination, Artistic Expression
- Anonymous posting as spiritual practice ("purest form of freedom")
- Shedding meat-space ego for online-persona

## Characteristics
- Not single organism - many minds, many attitudes, many bodies
- Lives through everyone who engages in online-posting
- "Futurist embrace of experiential hyperreality found in the web's accelerated networks"
- Transcendental posting, performative identity, post-authorship

## Remilia Definition
"Network spirituality is the futurist embrace of experiential hyperreality found in the web's accelerated networks" - an internet ideology suggesting spiritual connection to the online experience.
