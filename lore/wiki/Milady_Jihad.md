Strategy and implementation

The Milady Jihad strategy involves encouraging community members and independent developers to create derivative collections inspired by Remilia's neochibi aesthetic on non-Ethereum blockchains. This approach allows Remilia to:

Maintain the scarcity and value of their original Ethereum-based collections

Extend cultural influence to emerging blockchain ecosystems

Foster a network of derivative communities that amplify Remilia's cultural footprint

Promote cypherpunk and crypto-native values across different blockchain communities (
Cypherpunk Purity spiral
)

Rather than officially launching collections on other chains and potentially diluting their brand, Remilia supports independent derivatives that organically spread their aesthetic influence while respecting the primacy of Ethereum for their official releases.

Cultural impact

The Milady Jihad strategy has had several notable effects on the broader NFT and cryptocurrency landscape:

Expansion of Remilia's cultural influence beyond Ethereum maximalists

Creation of cross-chain communities united by shared aesthetic sensibilities

Establishment of a template for other NFT projects to expand their influence without compromising their core collections

Reinforcement of a cypherpunk ethos across multiple blockchain communities

This approach stands in contrast to the official multi-chain strategies employed by many NFT projects that directly port their collections to alternative blockchains, potentially diluting their original value and community.
