Milady Rave London: Resurrected
 was an early installment in what would later become the 
33reisen
 rave series, held on December 16, 2022, in Walthamstow, London. Organized by 
Remilia Corporation
, this event was the second Remilia rave in London, hence the name "Resurrected," and predated the formal establishment of the 33reisen branding.
