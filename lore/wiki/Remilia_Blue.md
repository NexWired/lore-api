Remilia Blue
 is a standardized corporate color specification used by 
Remilia Corporation
 in digital design and branding, defined by the hexadecimal color code #0000FF (RGB: 0, 0, 255).

[
1
]

 The color represents maximum blue saturation in the RGB color space, with no red or green components.

The specification was first documented in materials for the 
I Long For Network Spirituality
 exhibition (2021), though earlier implementations appeared on 
XCELA Group
 websites.

[
2
]

 The choice of pure digital blue draws conceptual inspiration from 

International Klein Blue

 (IKB), the ultramarine pigment developed by French artist Yves Klein in the 1960s, translating Klein's material experimentation into the RGB color space.

Technical specifications

Remilia Blue is defined by the following color values:

Hexadecimal
: #0000FF

RGB
: 0, 0, 255

Color space
: sRGB

Saturation
: 100%

Brightness
: 100%

The specification uses maximum blue channel intensity while setting red and green channels to zero, producing a pure primary blue in digital displays.

Usage

The color serves as the official blue standard across 
Remilia Corporation
's digital design, including web design, promotional materials, digital interfaces. The standardization ensures visual consistency across the organization's various projects and releases.
