Projects

Shishi

Shishi520

Shishi520 is a collection of 3,520 generative NFTs in a neochibi aesthetic with randomized traits inspired by net art and fashion trends.

[
3
]

 The collection draws on Japanese fashion, Korean fashion, and Y2K aesthetic influences, and was described as Shiro's first generative collection inspired by custom profile picture artwork.

[
4
]

The project aims to combine whitehearted and whitepilled sentiments with net art and fashion design, reflecting Shiro's background in fashion and her engagement with internet culture aesthetics.

Eth Chan, day twenty-two, June 7, 2025

Eth Chan

Shiro created the Eth Chan character as part of an ongoing art series titled "Day X of drawing eth chan until 10k Ethereum."

[
5
]

 The series features daily artwork of the anime-style character Eth Chan, who serves as a mascot for Ethereum and represents hope for the cryptocurrency reaching $10,000 in value.

The project gained significant attention within the Ethereum community, including recognition from Ethereum co-founder Vitalik Buterin, who followed Shiro on social media.

[
6
]

 The character's popularity led to multiple unauthorized memecoin launches using the Eth Chan character, which Shiro has publicly opposed.

Affiliation with Remilia

Shiro is a princess of Remilia. Shiro has expressed public support for Remilia Corporation, describing herself as believing in "Remilia One Thousand Forever" in social media posts.

[
7
]

 Her work shares aesthetic connections with Remilia projects through the use of neochibi styling, Japanese fashion influences, and internet culture references similar to collections like Milady Maker.
