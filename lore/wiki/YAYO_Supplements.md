YAYO Supplements

YAYO Supplements
 is the health and lifestyle product division of 
YAYO Corporation
, focused on testosterone optimization, athletic performance, and libido enhancement. The brand represents YAYO's evolution from digital assets into physical products, positioning itself around themes of health, fitness, financial success, and psychological self-programming.

Development

YAYO Supplements conducted a test launch in August 2023, releasing pine pollen powder as its flagship product. This limited trial run was accompanied by the "Trapezoid Scheme," an affiliate sales program that humorously alluded to pyramid schemes and featured MLM-style satirical salesforce bootcamps held in YAYO's Discord community server.
