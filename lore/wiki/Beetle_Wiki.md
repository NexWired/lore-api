 is a community-maintained wiki focused on documenting 
BeetleCraft
—the crafting system added to the beetle game on 
RemiliaNET
 during the 
RemiliaNET Alpha v0.5: Christmas Crafting Update
.

Background and history

BeetleCraft, introduced in RemiliaNET v0.5, expanded beetlegaming into a two-mode crafting tree (
Assemble
 and 
Smash
) with unrecorded recipes intended to be discovered through experimentation and shared (or withheld) socially.

[
3
]

Beetle Wiki’s revision log indicates the 
Start
 page was created on 25 December 2025 by the pseudonymous contributor 
Harblinger
.

[
4
]

Scope and structure

The wiki is organized around the primary BeetleCraft knowledge objects and practical play notes, including:

Beetles
 (variant documentation and reference pages).

[
5
]

Crafted items and recipes
 (assembly/smash outputs and ingredient combinations).

[
6
]

Pro tips
 collected from chat-based community discussion and field observations.

[
7
]

Meta / governance notes
 documenting documentation standards, plugins, and planned formatting improvements.

8

Documentation practices

A meta discussion page describes early-stage standardization work (including tagging and presentation tooling) and characterizes the project as intentionally iterative and “chaotic at first,” reflecting the partial and shifting nature of discovered BeetleCraft information in active play.

9
]

Relationship to other Remilia documentation

Beetle Wiki operates as a specialized documentation site alongside broader Remilia archival efforts, including the official 
Remilia Internet Culture Encyclopedia
 (Remilia Wiki) announced on 1 January 2026.
