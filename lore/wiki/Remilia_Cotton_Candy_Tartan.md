Remilia Cotton Candy Tartan
 is a tartan pattern designed by 
Remilia Corporation
 and registered in the Scottish Register of Tartans on February 13, 2024, under reference number 14208.

 The pattern was created for the 
33reisen
 Spring/Summer 2024 concept collection, 
Reisen Academy
, and functions as a brand identifier for Remilia Atelier, the organization's fashion line.

[
2

The design was intended to serve as a visual signifier for the Reisen Academy concept line and was debuted at the 33reisen rave series events in 2024.

 The pattern was officially registered with the Scottish Register of Tartans, a national repository of tartan designs maintained by the National Records of Scotland, established under the Scottish Register of Tartans Act 2008.

Design concept

The creation of Remilia Cotton Candy Tartan drew inspiration from the use of tartan in fashion branding, particularly Vivienne Westwood's integration of tartan into punk fashion and Burberry's use of tartan as a corporate identifier. The design was conceived to exist outside traditional clan and institutional tartan associations while maintaining immediate recognizability as a brand element.

[
5
]

The tartan serves as what the organization describes as a reversal of Westwood's approach to punk, with 
Remilia Corporation
 creating brand signifiers simultaneously with the subculture itself rather than deriving them from an existing movement.

Registration and usage

The tartan is registered under the fashion category in the Scottish Register of Tartans with commercial use restrictions requiring designer approval. This protection differs from 
Remilia Corporation
's typical approach to intellectual property, which generally favors open licensing.
