Background

Kerosene established herself within London's underground breakcore music scene, performing as a DJ while also developing a following as a cosplay influencer.

[
2
]

 Breakcore, a high-energy electronic music genre characterized by rapid tempos, distorted breakbeats, and experimental sound design, emerged from European underground scenes in cities including London in the mid-1990s.

[
3
]

The Nazi Anorexia Cult

In Fall 2021, Kerosene faced an online cancellation campaign after critics discovered controversial content she had liked on her private Twitter account, including a "mongolian george floyd" meme and thinspo (thinspiration) content related to eating disorders.

[
4
]

Rather than apologizing, Kerosene participated in an elaborate deception orchestrated by 
Sonya Qafi
 (a persona of 
Charlotte Fang
) in which she claimed her controversial online behavior had resulted from being manipulated by a fictional cult operating under the hashtag #KALIACC.

[
5
]

 According to Fang's retrospective account, "Heavy attention was drummed up on Instagram with her deflecting her cancel by blaming her anorexia and racism on this supposed cult."

[
6
]

The hoax gained traction when a new Instagram account called "hellokittyrazorblade" appeared, purporting to gather testimonies from other "victims" escaping the fabricated cult. After approximately two weeks, the account revealed the operation as a "sociological study" orchestrated by Miya (another persona associated with Fang). Following this reveal, Kerosene's secondary Instagram account was renamed "miya.cyber.angel.baby" and shared a summary of the operation.

Association with Remilia Corporation
