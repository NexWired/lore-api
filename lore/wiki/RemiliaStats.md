RemiliaStats
 is a community-run website that displays leaderboards and user profile statistics for 
RemiliaNET
. It was created and is maintained by the pseudonymous developer 
Douvy
.

Background

On 10 October 2025, Douvy announced RemiliaStats as a RemiliaNET leaderboard intended to track “important stats,” with additional features planned over time.

[
1
]

Features

RemiliaStats provides ranked leaderboards and per-account profile pages showing RemiliaNET statistics.

[
2
]

Community use

Users have referenced RemiliaStats in posts sharing leaderboard placements (for example, beetle ranks “out of 5570 users”).
