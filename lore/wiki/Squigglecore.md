# Squigglecore

Source: https://wiki.remilia.org/Squigglecore

A representative example of Squigglecore in blockchain art.

Squigglecore
 is a critical term coined by 
Charlotte Fang
 in 2025 to describe a category of generative blockchain art characterized by abstract, algorithm-generated patterns. The term specifically references "Chromie Squiggle" by Snowfro, a foundational project on the Art Blocks platform, and uses it to critique a broader aesthetic movement within NFT fine art.

Squigglecore
 refers to generative art projects on blockchain platforms that emphasize algorithmic aesthetics. According to Fang, these works represent an approach to blockchain art that focuses on technical implementation rather than deeper engagement with the medium's social and financial dimensions. The term is used within Fang's theoretical framework to contrast conventional approaches to blockchain art with what would come to be known as 
Avant NFT
, which focuses on crypto's potential for creating new relationships between artists, audiences, and markets.

Origin

The term directly references "Chromie Squiggle" by artist Snowfro (Erick Calderon), one of the earliest and most iconic generative art projects on the Art Blocks platform. Launched in 2020, Chromie Squiggle became emblematic of the generative art movement within NFTs and achieved significant status within blockchain art circles.
