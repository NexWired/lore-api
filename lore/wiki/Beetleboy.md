# Beetleboy

Source: https://wiki.remilia.org/Beetleboy

Beetleboy

Beetleboy
 is a hybrid gacha-idle browser game integrated into 
RemiliaNET
, the social identity platform developed by 
Remilia Corporation
. Launched in September 2025 alongside RemiliaNET's alpha release, the game functions as a gamified engagement system where players collect beetles of varying rarities and unlock corresponding "beetle girl" character cards.

Interface and structure

Beetleboy presents as a diegetic physical console interface reminiscent of 1990s-2000s era handheld gaming devices, evoking PC-98 and Game Boy aesthetics. The system operates through a modular architecture where different cartridge modules connect to the base console, with each module providing distinct gameplay functions.

Gameplay

Catch Module

The Catch Module provides two methods of obtaining beetles: claiming and hunting.

Claim

Claims operate on a two-hour cooldown and guarantee one beetle per use. This function provides a consistent, no-cost method for players to expand their beetle collection.

Hunt

Hunting uses a charge-based system with three available hunts that regenerate over 90 minutes. Individual hunts impose a 30-minute cooldown that stacks cumulatively—using all three charges results in a full 90-minute cooldown period, while partial usage allows charges to regenerate as the cooldown timer decreases below 60 minutes. Each hunt costs 20-30 cheese and yields beetles of varying rarity tiers.

When players discover a new beetle species through either claiming or hunting, they unlock a corresponding beetle girl character card in their inventory. Each beetle girl represents its beetle species through anthropomorphized cosplay designs combining monster girl and mecha girl aesthetics, drawn in an oekaki style using pen and colored marker techniques.

Universal Basic Cheese Module

The Universal Basic Cheese (UBC) Module provides a daily cheese claim that serves as the primary currency acquisition method. Consecutive daily claims build a streak bonus that increases the daily cheese amount, with returns estimated between 200-500 cheese depending on streak length. This system allows players to perform approximately 10-15 hunts daily through regular engagement.

BeetleCraft Module

On December 24th, 2025, The Christmas Crafting Update introduced the BeetleCraft module, a crafting system with two togglable modes: Assemble and Smash. The update added 9 new beetle species including two limited-time holiday beetles, 14 flower types, and numerous collectible items with a six-tier rarity system. Quality of life improvements included a drag-and-drop crafting interface, color-coded item rarities, and item tooltips.

Pity system

The v0.2 update introduced a pity counter to address extended streaks of common green beetles. The system automatically elevates beetle rolls to higher rarity tiers after excessive common results, resetting upon activation. The developers described this mechanic as preventing "green hell."

Seasonal content

Skull Bug Girl, a limited-time seasonal beetle girl for Halloween 2025

Beetleboy incorporates limited-time seasonal variations. The Halloween 2025 update added two holiday-themed beetles and beetle girls available exclusively during the two-week Halloween period, establishing a pattern for future seasonal releases.

Integration with RemiliaNET

Beetleboy serves multiple functions within RemiliaNET's ecosystem beyond entertainment. Progress in the game determines access to platform features, with Level 10 serving as the threshold for global chat access.

[
4
]

 The module was characterized in design documentation as a "global chat fidget spinner" designed for modular expansion as later gameplay stages unlock.

[
1
]

The game exemplifies RemiliaNET's gamified anti-sybil approach, using organic gameplay engagement as an alternative to invasive identity verification. Beetle collection and interaction patterns contribute to the platform's broader social credit scoring system.
