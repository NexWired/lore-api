Development

Nochillio was created by artist 
thetopsignal
 (also known as 
Nochillionaire
) along with core members of the 
NO CHILL
 project providing social and technical support. The development took several months with thetopsignal experimenting with aesthetics and traits to find the appropriate balance necessary for adoption within the Avalanche ecosystem.

Launch

Nochillio was launched on July 8, 2024

[
2
]

, minting 1337 NFTs on the Avalanche blockchain as ERC-721 tokens. The collection was deployed to contract address 0x204b3EE3f9bDCDe258BA3F74dE76ea8Eedf0A36A.

Users were able to mint up to two NFTs per wallet at a price of 2.222 AVAX. A whitelist period was offered that lasted one hour. During the whitelist period, users could mint one nochillio NFT for 1.337 AVAX.

Community and Ecosystem

Nochillio exists within the broader Milady and Remilia derivative ecosystem. It is the only Remilia derivative existing on the Avalanche blockchain. It is heavily tied to the NOCHILL memecoin that exists on Avalanche, which was launched several months prior to the NFT collection (on December 18, 2023)
