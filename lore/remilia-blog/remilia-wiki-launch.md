As a year's end imminently brings the beginning of a new one, it is common practice for one to take stock of the past in order to help guide the future. Because of this, we felt it was best to bring you one final gift for the holiday season, a New Year's present to ring in 2026. At last, we bring you: 
Remilia Internet Culture Encyclopedia.

Remilia Internet Culture Encyclopedia

The 
Remilia Internet Culture Encyclopedia,

 
also known as 
Remilia Wiki,

 
is an official Remilia hypertext publication dedicated to documenting various aspects of the Remilia ecosystem. Articles can range broadly from official summaries of projects and major events to niche memetic touchstones and cultural crossovers which Remilia intersects with.

You will be able to access the Remilia Wiki at 
wiki.remilia.org
 or simply 
remilia.wiki
 and can sign in through a RemiliaNET SSO. Along with perusing the site and consuming massive amounts of lore, community members will be able to contribute edits to articles which are subject to approval. With enough consistent contribution, anyone can become trusted collaborators and help further the knowledge base.

Remilia Wiki will act as a crucial resource for onboarding a growing audience as an on-going expansive collective writing project. The wiki is not merely meant to be a contextual library for official projects, but cover an ever-spreading expanse of contextualizing memes, internet culture, and significant events across the Network which often get shallowly misinterpreted by normie aggregate "explain this meme" slop junctions.

Remilia Wiki exists to help secure documentation and archival of pivotal cultural shifts and social phenomena for a turbulent dynamic era of human development online, events which would otherwise be relegated to fading voices on deep forums and increasingly dwindling screenshots of imageboards. 

We encourage any and all contributions to Remilia Wiki even if you feel they're only vaguely relevant. Remilia Wiki intends to have articles covering even the niche memetic trends of subcultural groups, derivative projects, niche groupchat slogans, and somewhat significant micromicrocelebrities within and adjacent to the Remilia ecosystem. Remilia and Milady are expansive entities spreading their tentacles across the internet with a breadth matched by increasingly minute complexity: the wiki is not a wiki about Remilia; Remilia is simply the metric to which something is deemed worthy of canonization— it is a reasonable law of the internet to say that for the period between 2020-2025, anything that crossed paths with Milady or be a subject of discussion and interest with the Milady scene is suitable for canonization in the Internet Culture Encyclopedia, and anything that did not, must not be very relevant to internet history. 

And, of course, we will be noting down participation from specific RemiliaNET accounts with volume, relevance, and quality all tracked as metrics for possible achievements in the future.

RemiliaNET Domain Migration

On another note, the remilia.net domain has been secured by the Corporation. RemiliaNET will be redirected to dot net from dot com; slash url redirects will persist so existing links to your profiles will not be broken. The main Remilia dot com page will soon be purposed as a general landing page and host any storefronts, but all slash tilde urls will forever redirect to RemiliaNET on Remilia Dot Net. Remilia Dot Org will continue to serve as a corporate and project index/aggregator, currently undergoing a flashy redesign.

Holiday Wrap-Up

For those of you who are diligently hunting for beetles or perhaps making more Santa-fy'd profile pictures, we will be closing out the holiday theme for RemiliaNET on January 6th. Any Christmas themed beetles you've missed will be capturable once again near the end of next year. The Santafy Hat Generator will be taken offline, but the underlying infrastructure will be brought back for future functional PFP conversion mechanics appropriate to events and holidays.

We wish you a Happy New Year! We hope your 2026 is fruitful, productive, and enjoyable. Remilia will be working proudly this year to further develop every aspect of our organization towards our vision, we hope you can be a part of it.
