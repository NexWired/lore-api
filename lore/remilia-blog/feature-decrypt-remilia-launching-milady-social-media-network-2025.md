September 19, 2025

Decrypt covered the launch of our RemiliaChat social media initiative, examining how we're building alternative social infrastructure to address shortcomings in existing platforms. Writer Ryan S. Gladwin explored our approach to creating community-driven social media experiences and the technical innovation behind our social credit scoring system.

Platform Overview

The coverage details our two-phase approach: RemiliaNET as the initial social identity layer, followed by the full RemiliaChat platform. As Chief of Staff Michael Dragovic explained to Decrypt: "RemiliaNET is the identity service layer of Remilia Chat. We're launching it now prior to releasing full social features to begin the onboarding process."

Content Moderation Approach

The coverage addresses our nuanced approach to content moderation, balancing free discourse principles with practical platform management needs. The article notes our design-focused moderation philosophy where "90% of moderation comes from the way you design the layout of your site."

Cultural Context

Decrypt positions our initiative within broader conversations about social media alternatives and the "diaspora of chan culture," recognizing our community's deep roots in internet culture and dissatisfaction with mainstream platforms.

Market Differentiation

The article explores how our approach differs from existing social media platforms by:

Emphasizing anonymity and pseudonymity over real-name identity

Using achievement-based rather than algorithmic engagement metrics

Integrating blockchain-based rewards and identity verification

Focusing on quality discourse over viral content

Development Process

The coverage highlights our experimental approach, noting that we've "experimented with dozens of platforms in the past five years" to understand what makes platforms successful and sustainable.

Our Perspective

We appreciate Decrypt's recognition of the sophisticated technical and cultural framework underlying our social media initiative. The coverage accurately captures our vision of creating gathering spaces for "radical thought" and "hard online discourse principles."

The article's attention to both our technical innovation and cultural mission demonstrates understanding of how we're building infrastructure that serves our community's specific needs while potentially influencing broader internet culture.

Future Implications

The coverage positions RemiliaChat as part of a broader movement toward decentralized, community-controlled social infrastructure, with potential implications for how online communities organize and communicate.
