an unsung masterpiece of new urbanism

Here in Irvine CA, we'll attract talent to expand our development studio, including with an in-house AI research lab. 

We will also be expanding our existing office in Hongdae, Seoul, which houses our growing fashion, design and illustration teams.

We knew from the start Remilia is playing a decade long game to win the culture war, and it’s this coming year we go on the offensive.

The macro timing could not be more perfect, as the Trump Mandate vibe shift confirmed our out-of-the-money bets placed on the outside edges of the Overton Window will pay off: the pendulum has now swung back into our corner, leaving the cancelled to inherit the earth... just as prophesied.

But for now, let’s take the time to set down arms and enjoy peace this Christmas. May this time strengthen our bond and connection that we all share across the network.

Here's to abundance, love and victory in the coming year—and to a thousand year dynasty beyond 🥂.
