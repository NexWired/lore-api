RemiliaNET Alpha v0.6: Streaming, Announcements, and Events

        

        

            
Jan 17, 2026

            
        

        

            

                
RemiliaNET's newest update is a series of features developed to help integrate real-life participation at Remilia's live events with the Network experience that occurs simultaneously for thousands of Remilia enthusiasts. 

Delivered just in time for the upcoming 
FRUITS X REMILIA TOKYO 2026
 rave held in Shinjuku on January 17th, this update features the proper introduction to our new announcements system, a QR-code check-in database for tracking user attendance, and a livestream telecast module for RemiNET users to enjoy the rave alongside attendants.

Announcements

The announcements shoutbox will appear for every new rave or event, future major updates, product releases, and any other notable happenings which demand the community's attention. The adjacent ticker scrolling the top of the page may contain countdown timers and can be hidden by clicking an "x" in the top right, revealed again by clicking the event in the notifications tab.

RemiliaNET announcements will now be a centralized reliable source of news and information coming directly from Remilia Corporation, uninhibited by the limitations, censorship, or logistic disturbance of third party applications. 

RemiCast

You'll notice a new tab in the activity window next to BeetleBoy, called RemiCast. This is where you can watch live feeds from Remilia raves and events, scheduled livestreams, or even just official watch parties for any kind of content Remilia wishes to show you. Global chat is active and adjacent so you can share the experience with other RemiliaNET users now. We intend to utilize RemiCasts for all of our future event livestreams.

QR Code Check-In

RemiliaNET users will now be able to check-in their attendance to Remilia raves and other live events with the QR code from their profile ID card. Pressing the card on mobile pulls up a more visible QR code and event staff can scan it, marking the profile as having attended the event in our in-house database. Shortly after, you'll get a notification confirming you've checked in and your attendance will appear in the activity feed as well. Marking live attendance will help us collect data for meaningful participation with the Remilia ecosystem and users will be receive for future achievements based on their attendance for certain events.

General Updates

Decoration Cleanup

The holiday theme and respective Christmas beetles have been retired this week, ready to return this December for all those who missed out on catching their own special beetles. The SantaFy Christmas Hat generator has also been pulled back into the maintenance room to be converted into something different for a near future update.

Fixes and Improvements

Fixed profile card tilt animation on hover

Patched admin panel glitch related to BeetleCraft inventory

Streamlined database to a more efficient configuration

Implemented MongoDB client integration to Go monolith for future data consolidation

Improved frontend state management for much better handling and load time

Profile management endpoints now use APIv2 instead of legacy API routes

Multiple linting passes and code cleanup across frontend and backend

We will continue to add more features and integrations to RemiliaNET that reward our community for acts of loyalty and participation, both on and offline. Thank you for continuing to grow with us and have an excellent weekend.
