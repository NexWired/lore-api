Beetleboy Updates

The Beetleboy module has its own team dedicated to active ongoing development of new characters, mechanics, and cartridge gamemodes designed to evolve the overall Beetleboy game meta. We are actively monitoring player data and social media commentary to maximize both the addicting appeal and social coordination opportunities afforded by our ever-expanding gachamaxxing fidget toy. 

Many of you have already noticed our active tinkering with the Beetle economy. This initial update consists of a collected patch responding to feedback and the addition of several new Beetlegirls.

Beetleboy Patch Notes:

Only one beetle can now be caught at a time in claims and hunts, reduced from three

Base Cheese Claim rate has been doubled

Beetle Hunt's "no beetle" hitrate has been reduced

Cheese Claim streak bonus has been adjusted to a more balanced curve

Pity-counter introduced to Beetle Hunt, preventing “green hell”: when a user reaches an excessive streak of common-tier Green Beetles, they’ll automatically get a green beetle roll bumped into a "pity roll" for a higher-tier beetle, upon which the counter resets

Beetle Hunt cheese cost has been reduced to 20 cheese per hunt down from week 2's 30 (and up from launch week's 10) upon the successful cessation of the Great Beetle Strike of 2025, through tenuous negotiation with the Beetleboy Union

Cheese icon in Cheese Claim module has now been replaced by The Cheeseman 

RemiliaNET fixes and Improvements

Every bit of feedback provided by the community is being diligently processed by our development team which works round the clock to ensure your experience on RemiliaNET is constantly improving. We thank our loyal and lovable users for their enthusiasm, support, and energy as we continue to polish RemiliaNET in tandem with building our classified, highly ambitious multi-year (multi-decade, and multi-century) roadmap. Mobile:

Resolved mobile profile tab responsive animation issues

Fixed mobile search results display and responsiveness

Fixed Safari prompting users to download a login file 

 Beetleboy:

Resolved game buttons being clickable when disconnected

Stats Tracking:

Corrected Miladycraft stats tracking integration

Twitter data collection steadily catching up to all connected users (initial delay induced by mass influx of users upon launch, ongoing data collection will be much faster via a team of interns manually scrolling through your Twitter feed and counting your “milady” replies by hand)

Fixed notification overflow limiting UI display
