# Network Spirituality - Explained
*Source: networkspirits.ltd/spirit*
*Scraped: 2026-02-06*

## Origin

"Network Spirituality" was first coined by FODCOM, a Remilia Corporation artist, and widely introduced with Remilia's show "I Long for Network Spirituality."

Remilia did not invent Network Spirituality — they "watered the soil" where such ideologies grow, deep within "The Wired."

## The Wired (Serial Experiments Lain, 1998)

The Wired is a virtual realm containing the sum of all human communication and networks — created with telegraph, television, telephone, and expanded with Internet/cyberspace.

The series assumes the Wired could link to a system enabling unconscious communication between people and machines without physical interface — like a spiritual network. If created, the network would become equivalent to reality as the general consensus of all perceptions and knowledge.

Written by Chiaki J. Konaka in 1998.

## Pierre Teilhard de Chardin's Noosphere

Philosopher and Jesuit priest. His concept of Noosphere ties directly to Network Spirituality:

- Both theorize life will reach total unification (Lain: society + technology; de Chardin: the universe)
- Both explore consciousness and awareness
- Noosphere = highest stage of biospheric development
- Human reason and scientific thought create the next evolutionary geological layer
- As mankind organizes in more complex social networks, the noosphere grows in awareness

## The Internet as Evolutionary Step

- The internet is a new collective behavior and complex social network
- Creates social processes that don't reflect existing social structures
- Millions born in different eras, conjugating on the network hive
- Choice to be whoever you'd like, say whatever you'd like, post whatever you'd like
- No longer living through the ego the corrupt system warped for you
- Being anonymous online = purest form of freedom today
- Anonymous/online-persona = being born again

## Collective Consciousness

Teilhard: "evolution requires a unification of consciousness."

When we become collectively conscious that:
- Our internal and external existence is spiritually unified with the network
- Our mind and body are egoless
- The content we create is with Truth and Beauty

...we become Network Spiritual.

## Practices

Disrupting normative conscious embodiment with Altered States of Consciousness induces minds more open to perceiving complex dynamics of adaptive system networks.

Practices:
- Psychotherapy
- Mindfulness Meditations
- Dreaming
- Hypnogogic States
- Active Imagination
- Artistic Expressions

Doing these on a complex social network system = practicing Network Spirituality.

## Definition

"Network spirituality is the futurist embrace of experiential hyperreality found in the web's accelerated networks" — an internet ideology suggesting spiritual connection to the online experience.

Through anonymous online-personas, shedding meat-space ego:
- Transcendental posting
- Performative identity
- Post-authorship

Fully removing Ego from online-personas = angelic levels of posting.
