# Network Spirits - Art Origin Story
*Source: networkspirits.ltd/art*
*Scraped: 2026-02-06*

## Creator's Journey

The creator considers themselves spiritual. Upon joining Milady/Remilia community, noticed "Network Spirituality" being discussed in tweets.

Key insight: **Posting is an art form within itself.** People in the community treat it like Art, not posting just for posting sake.

## The Photo Inspiration

A particular photo Charlotte Fang and others used in posts — a Chinese girl seeming to ask a question. Very brute, up in your face, but innocent in posture. Perfect for memes.

This became the overall inspiration for the project.

## Waaaladys Connection

Community members started making Milady face edits — zoomed in, eyes forward, looking directly at the viewer. This style became the Waaaladys NFT collection.

Waaaladys succeeded because:
- Very memeable
- Great for posting
- Cool as PFP
- Simple but fresh
- Created 1-of-1 editions for community members

## Network Spirits Art Evolution

1. Started stretching/bending Milady faces in Procreate
2. Added different mouths
3. People asked for their Miladys in the same style
4. Remembered "Ring Camera Doorbell" view idea
5. Combined with Chinese girl stance
6. Redrew all facial features in layers for collection potential

## The Phrase Origin

Created an honorary for Remikun (@hodlboi). That tweet was the first appearance of:

**"Can I interest you in Network Spirituality?"**

The phrase felt right — matching the questioning, interested pose.

## Why This Name

- Network Spirituality is present in daily lives but unseen/unbelieved
- Remilia spreads awareness of Network Spirituality
- Milady as vessel introducing you to Network Spirituality
- "In a polite but in your face manner in the form of a question"

## Ring Camera Aesthetic

Added Ring Camera views as backgrounds — now appears as if Milady is at your door asking if you're interested in Network Spirituality, "like some kind of religion evangelist."

## Philosophy

> "When we consciously engage with the network both internally and externally, egoless, filled with love, truth, beauty and awareness, we are spiritually connecting."

Created because of love for Remilia and how it expanded consciousness:
- Create without fear of judgement (online and in life)
- Never allow ego to make decisions
- Expression of individuality is meaningful pleasure driving evolution

Sold for pennies — wanted to make people spiritually aware of the Network hive and reap rewards of digital interconnectedness.

## Charlotte Fang Connection

Charlotte Fang recognized the meme potential of the work.
