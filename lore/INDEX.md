### Network Spirituality
Online participation as genuine spiritual practice. The wired is not secondary to "real life" — it IS real life.

### Post-Authorship
Individual authorship obsolete. Remix is natural mode. Attribution matters less than proliferation.

### The Whitepill
Anti-nihilism, return to transcendentalism. Radical love through the end times.

### The Wired Eats the Real
Threshold where cultural legitimacy flows from network-native creation rather than institutions.

### Human Non-Agency
The blackpill of accelerationism: humans cannot steer technological development. Capitalism is "the thermodynamic ruling entity of all material dynamics."

### Neochibi / Lefthand Style
Deliberately childlike, amateurish art that rejects technical polish. Democratizes artistic participation.
